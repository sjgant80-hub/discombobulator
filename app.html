    Why re-grow instead of lift-and-shift?

    A byte-identical copy of a legacy system reproduces its undocumented flaws and locks you into the same black box. Re-growing extracts what the system does as rules that survive held-out data, shows you exactly where it can't reproduce the old behavior (so a human confirms the tribal rules in minutes), and leaves you with a small model you own and can re-verify — not another vendor dependency. Nothing uploads; this page has no server.
