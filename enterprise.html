Stop renting your core business logic.

Standard generative AI is probabilistic — it guesses. In core banking, logistics and regulated work, a guess is a liability. The Discombobulator reads your legacy system, extracts the business logic as rules, and keeps only the rules that predict your own historical decisions. What it cannot verify, it surfaces — it does not guess. You re-grow a sovereign asset you own outright, in your browser, with zero cloud.

The enterprise reality

"Lift and shift" recreates decades of brittle logic (COBOL, mainframe, monolithic Node/SQL) in a more expensive, un-sovereign wrapper — and a probabilistic LLM translating your core logic simply guesses at the rules nobody documented. The gnarly, undocumented 20% — the tribal rules — is exactly where guessing is catastrophic.

The neurosymbolic solution

It is not a chatbot, a copilot, or a migration wrapper. It separates the discovery of business logic from the certainty of it.

1
Neural proposalA Pattern Organ reads your legacy software, databases and decision logs and proposes the business intent as candidate rules. The neural layer only proposes.
2
Symbolic verification — the gateEach proposed rule is compiled to a symbolic rule and tested against your own held-out history. If it cannot predict what your system actually did, it is rejected. The engine abstains rather than hallucinates, and every mismatch is surfaced and attributed to a candidate rule for a human to confirm in minutes.

Sovereign re-growthThe verified rules are compressed to a small, auditable, readable pattern book — not bloated neural weights. It germinates a structurally superior asset you own: a runnable replacement module plus a signed migration receipt.
